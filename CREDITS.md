# Credits

Character art comes from the [Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator) (commit `86a3719ee4a7e2e9fb27c7c152e29a741d3e930a`), which bundles Liberated Pixel Cup assets by many artists. Only the walk animation is used. Some sheets are recoloured (skin tones, neutral grey for tinting) and the body and head are merged into one sheet per skin tone. These derivatives remain under the original licences (CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0 / CC0 as listed per asset).
